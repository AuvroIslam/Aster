# Aster — Features

## 1. YouTube Accessibility Learning

- **Adaptive Visual Explanation**  
  Aster listens to the creator's narration and pauses the video only when important visual information needs to be explained.

- **Full AI Video Explanation**  
  Aster can explain the entire video in an audio-first way, describing diagrams, code, graphs, formulas, slides, and other visual content so a visually impaired learner can understand the lesson.

- **Interactive Video Q&A**  
  Students can ask questions about the current video, such as “Explain this diagram,” “Read the code,” or “What changed?”

- **Whole-Video Understanding**  
  Aster understands the video's overall topic and context before explaining individual visual moments.

- **Multilingual Learning**  
  Aster can explain lessons in the language of the original content, including Bengali and English.

- **Voice Video Search**  
  Students can search for educational YouTube videos using their voice.

## 2. Interactive AI Tutor

- **Context-Aware Questions**  
  Students can ask Aster questions about what they are currently learning.

- **Concept Re-Explanation**  
  Aster can explain difficult concepts again using simpler language, examples, or step-by-step explanations.

- **Personalized Learning**  
  Aster adapts explanations to the student's questions and learning context.

## 3. PDF & Notes Learning

- **Accessible Document Understanding**  
  Upload PDFs, lecture notes, textbooks, or slides and Aster explains both their textual and visual content.

- **Visual Content Explanation**  
  Aster explains diagrams, graphs, tables, formulas, charts, and other visual information within study materials.

- **Document Q&A**  
  Students can ask questions about their uploaded materials and get context-aware answers.

- **AI-Generated Quizzes**  
  Aster creates quizzes from the student's study materials to help them practice and test their understanding.

## 4. Accessibility & Privacy

- **Audio-First Experience**  
  The entire learning experience is designed to work naturally with screen readers, keyboard controls, and spoken interaction.

- **Accessibility by Design**  
  High contrast, keyboard navigation, screen-reader support, large controls, adjustable speech settings, and reduced-motion support.

- **Local AI or free api such as Gemma**  
  Aster is designed to use local AI models, reducing dependence on cloud APIs and eliminating per-request AI API costs while keeping learning content more private.
