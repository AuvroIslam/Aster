1. 🎥 YouTube Accessibility Learning — Core

Adaptive audio description: Aster lets the creator's narration play naturally, then pauses only when important visual information needs explanation.

Full AI narration: Users can choose to have Aster explain the entire video in a way designed for a visually impaired learner—not simply read the transcript.


2. 🧑‍🏫 Interactive AI Tutor

Aster becomes a personal tutor that can answer questions, re-explain difficult concepts, and guide the learner through the lesson interactively.

3. 📄 PDF & Notes → Learning

Upload notes, textbooks, or PDFs; Aster understands the material, explains visual content accessibly, and generates quizzes for active learning.

The overall idea

> Aster transforms visual learning content into an interactive, audio-first learning experience for blind and low-vision students.



And this is a very clean product loop:

Watch → Understand → Ask → Practice
